Ollie OS Installer for Mac   (Apple chip: M1, M2, M3, M4 ... or Intel)

This makes a USB drive that starts Ollie OS on a Steam Deck, a Steam Machine,
a Dell or other PC, or an Intel Mac. A Mac with an Apple chip can make the
drive, but it cannot start Ollie OS itself.

Website: https://robertpjenkins-bot.github.io/Ollie-OS/
All versions: https://github.com/robertpjenkins-bot/Ollie-OS/releases

HOW IT WORKS (the same as the Linux and Windows installers)
  1. It looks in your Downloads folder for Ollie OS (ollie-os-...iso) first. If it
     is there, it goes straight to step 3.
  2. Otherwise it builds a brand-new Ollie OS on this Mac: it downloads the
     official Arch Linux and turns it into Ollie OS. This needs Docker Desktop
     (free, docker.com): install it and open it once before you start.
       Intel Mac: 1 to 2 hours.
       Apple chip (M1 to M4): several hours (Docker runs it emulated). In Docker
       Desktop, Settings > General, turn on "Use Rosetta". Leave the Mac on and
       plugged in. About 20 GB of free space is needed.
     The new Ollie OS goes into Downloads (and is found there next time).
  3. The USB Writer puts it on a USB drive (8 GB or more; EVERYTHING ON IT IS
     ERASED): pick the drive, type ERASE, and type this Mac's password.

START IT
  Double-click "Ollie OS Installer.command". macOS may say it "can't be opened"
  or "Apple could not verify" it, because it is not from the App Store. Then:
    1. Click Done (not Move to Bin).
    2. Open System Settings > Privacy & Security.
    3. Scroll down to the message about "Ollie OS Installer.command" and click
       Open Anyway, then type this Mac's password.
    4. Double-click it again and click Open.
  (macOS 14 and older: right-click it and choose Open instead.)
  macOS may ask whether Terminal may use the Downloads folder: click Allow.
  If macOS blocks writing to the drive: System Settings > Privacy & Security >
  Full Disk Access, turn on Terminal, quit Terminal (Command + Q), run it again.

QUICKER, NO BUILD (downloads a ready-made Ollie OS, about 2 GB, 10 to 30 minutes)
  Double-click "Ollie OS USB Writer.command", or open Terminal and paste:

  bash <(curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/robertpjenkins-bot/Ollie-OS/claude/fervent-gates-cs7smv/os/usb/mac/ollie-mac-install.sh)

STARTING FROM THE DRIVE
  Steam Deck:     hold Volume Down, press Power, pick the USB drive.
  Steam Machine:  hold Volume Down.
  Dell and most PCs: tap F12.
  Intel Mac:      hold Option and pick "EFI Boot".
  Then press Install Ollie OS.
  PCs with Secure Boot on: the first time, a blue screen says "Verification
  failed". Press Enter, then any key quickly, then pick Enroll key from disk,
  OLLIE_OS, OLLIE-OS-KEY.cer, Continue, Yes, Reboot. Only once on each computer.
  A PC with Windows: write down its BitLocker recovery key first
  (aka.ms/myrecoverykey). Windows may ask for it after starting from a USB.

If the Mac says "The disk you inserted was not readable by this computer" when
the drive is done, click Eject: that is Ollie OS, which a Mac cannot read.
