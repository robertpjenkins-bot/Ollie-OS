Ollie OS 1.7: Windows is out, and lots of new things

Windows is taken out of Ollie OS: its install apps, Restart into Windows and the Windows start-up menu entry are removed from this computer when you install this update. Your files, games and saves stay.

🌦️ School Planner: what to wear and pack, from the weather
↩️ Undo an update: Ollie OS saves itself before every update
🎨 Ollie Paint and 🖱️ Mouse Maker
🤖 No more robot voice, and new real icons
